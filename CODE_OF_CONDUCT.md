# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, evidence-seeking collaboration environment. Discuss claims and methods rather than impugning motives; disclose relevant conflicts; welcome corrections; and protect private personal information.

Harassment, threats, doxxing, discrimination, and knowingly fabricated evidence are not accepted. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers once contact details are published. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation to protect the community and the integrity of the research record.
