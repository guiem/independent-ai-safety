# Scope

## Purpose

Map institutions that materially work to reduce AI-related harm, with emphasis on organizations institutionally independent from frontier developers. The product is an auditable research artifact, not a ranking or promotional directory.

## Core records

A core organization must have a stable public identity, substantive ongoing or historically important safety work, and at least one reliable source establishing that work. Eligible forms include nonprofits, research institutes, distinct academic centers, public safety institutes, standards bodies, civil-society organizations, and third-party commercial evaluators.

Frontier model developers are never displayed as independent safety organizations. They may appear as context nodes when they fund, commission, collaborate with, or are evaluated by a core organization.

## Inclusion test

Include an organization when all are true:

1. It is an identifiable institution or stable fiscally sponsored project, not merely a community label or one-off event.
2. AI risk reduction is a material program supported by concrete outputs, services, statutory duties, or funding activity.
3. At least one primary or strong secondary source supports the description and classification.
4. Its status and relationship to parent/sponsor institutions can be represented without implying unsupported independence.

## Exclusions

- Generic “responsible AI” or “AI for good” claims without material safety work.
- Whole universities when a particular lab or center performs the work.
- Individual researchers, informal discussion groups, and one-off projects in the organization layer.
- Frontier developers as core independent organizations.
- Vendor listings whose only evidence is marketing language.

Inactive or merged institutions may remain for historical relationships but must be labeled and visually subdued.

## Context boundary

Context nodes include frontier developers, funders, fiscal sponsors/incubators, regulators, intergovernmental bodies, and major programs. They are hidden or subdued in the default view and cannot satisfy a “core organizations” filter.

## Geographic and conceptual coverage

The intended scope is global and spans catastrophic-risk, technical safety, evaluations, security/misuse, governance, human factors, present-day harms, standards, and public-interest accountability. Coverage will initially reflect English-language public sources; that limitation must be displayed rather than mistaken for absence.
